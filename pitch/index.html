01 / Problem
Casual games are massive, but their economies usually live outside the gameplay.
No real stakes

Most casual matches have no immediate economic layer attached to the outcome.

No trust layer

When players wager, settlement often depends on closed platforms or manual agreements.

No ownership

Cosmetics and power-ups rarely become transferable, player-owned assets.

02 / Solution

A PvP wager arcade built for Solana Mobile.

PingBlock combines fast ping pong matches, real-time matchmaking, and on-chain custody so two players can compete for a transparent prize pool.
